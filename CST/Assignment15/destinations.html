Tourist Destinations

1. Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, famous for mountains, snow, and adventure activities.

Best for: Nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

2. Goa

Goa is famous for its beaches, scenic sunsets, Portuguese heritage, and delicious food.

Best for: Beach lovers and relaxing holidays.

3. Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City of Rajasthan, is known for its forts, palaces, traditional markets, and culture.

Best for: History lovers and cultural explorers.
