5. College Terminology

Semester
A semester is one of the academic periods into which a college year is divided.
Lecture
A lecture is a teaching session in which a teacher explains a topic to students.
Assignment
An assignment is a task given to students to complete as part of their studies.
Practical
A practical is a learning session in which students apply theoretical knowledge through activities or experiments.
Attendance
Attendance refers to a student's presence in classes or other academic activities.
Examination
An examination is a test used to assess a student's knowledge and understanding.
